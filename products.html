Monstera Deliciosa

Quick Care Requirements

  • Sunlight: Bright Indirect Sunlight
  • Water: Keep soil moist but not waterlogged
  • Toxicity: Mildly Toxic to Pets (Calcium Oxalate)
  • Repotting: Every 12-18 months in rich, peat-based soil
